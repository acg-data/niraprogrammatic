Questions? We've got answers.

Frequently asked
questions.

How does Nira work?
Share your existing dental quote or treatment plan. We match you with vetted independent providers abroad, coordinate virtual consults, and handle travel logistics from start to finish.
How do payments and travel coordination work?
You pay clinics directly with full pricing transparency. Nira coordinates your flights, accommodations, and ground transport so everything is handled in one place.
Who provides treatment?
All treatment is delivered by licensed local dental professionals in vetted, accredited clinics. Nira is a coordination service and does not provide clinical care directly.
Is it safe to get dental work abroad?
When done with the right partners, yes. Our providers are credentialed, accredited, and use the same materials and standards you'd expect from premium clinics in the U.S.
What kind of dental work can I compare?
Major dental work includes veneers, crowns, implants, full-mouth restorations, and full smile makeovers. We focus on planned, comprehensive cases rather than emergencies.
What if I need follow-up care?
We coordinate post-treatment check-ins and can connect you with local dentists in the U.S. for follow-up needs. Our aftercare support continues long after your return.
